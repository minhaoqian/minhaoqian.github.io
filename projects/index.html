Short-Horizon Cross-Sectional Equity Alpha

Sep 2026 - Oct 2026 · Quantitative Research Project
  • Built a point-in-time US equity research pipeline using CRSP daily data from 1993–2025 to forecast five-trading-day cross-sectional returns, incorporating liquidity filters, corporate actions, walk-forward validation, and strict temporal controls.
  • Identified short-term reversal as the strongest standalone signal and validated its persistence in the final 2020–2025 predictive holdout, opened once after specifications were frozen, where mean Rank IC remained approximately 0.016 with positive average IC in all six calendar years and all five fixed non-overlapping phases.
  • Developed a quarterly expanding-window Ridge model using eight frozen daily features; the combination remained predictive but did not demonstrate incremental Rank-IC improvement over the simpler reversal signal in either development or holdout evaluation.
  • Extended the study into execution-aware market-neutral portfolio construction, explicitly modelling turnover, transaction costs, short borrowing and unresolved corporate-action states; measured trading frictions exceeded identified gross contribution under the pre-specified conditional implementation assumptions. Full-account wealth remained unidentified, so these results do not establish complete portfolio returns, Sharpe ratios or profitable net alpha.

Market Concentration and Cross-Sectional Momentum

Jun 2026 - Aug 2026 · Research Project
  • Built a reproducible empirical asset-pricing study using CRSP data from 2000–2025 to examine how rising US equity market concentration affects the implementation of cross-sectional momentum strategies.
  • Constructed 12–2 momentum signals, value- and equal-weighted long-short portfolios, company-level concentration measures, and HAC-based inference, with additional industry-neutral and ex-ante beta-neutral robustness tests.
  • Decomposed mega-cap exclusion effects into direct portfolio-weight and re-ranking channels, finding that the observed Ex-Top-10 return difference was driven almost entirely by portfolio weights rather than changes in the underlying momentum ranking.
  • Validated the implementation externally against the Kenneth French momentum factor and documented a strong relationship between aggregate market concentration and the winner-minus-loser mega-cap weight imbalance, while realised return effects remained statistically imprecise.

Time Series Forecasting — US Services Trade Balance

Apr 2026 · Academic Project
  • Analysed monthly US services trade balance data from 1992–2024 using time-series plots, differencing, and ACF/PACF diagnostics to assess stationarity and dependence.
  • Developed and refined ARIMA models through residual diagnostics and Ljung–Box testing, selecting an ARIMA(1,1,1) × (1,0,0)12 specification to capture short-run and annual dependence.
  • Produced 12-month forecasts with 95% prediction intervals and evaluated limitations including structural change, omitted macroeconomic drivers, and increasing forecast uncertainty.

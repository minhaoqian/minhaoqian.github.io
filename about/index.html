About

I am currently pursuing an MSc in Mathematical and Computational Finance at the University of Oxford, following a BSc in Statistics, Economics and Finance at University College London.

My academic interests lie primarily in quantitative finance, particularly where probability, statistics and computation meet financial markets. I am interested not only in how mathematical models are constructed, but also in how they behave when their assumptions meet real-world data.

Beyond academics, football has been a constant part of my university life. I also enjoy travelling, photography and keeping a record of the places, people and moments that have shaped these years.
